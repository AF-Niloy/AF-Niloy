## 👋 About Me

I'm **Abrar Faiyaz Niloy**, an Electrical and Electronic Engineering
student at **BRAC University**, majoring in **Electronics** with a minor
in **Power**.

I'm interested in understanding how hardware and software work
together---from electronic circuits and semiconductor devices to
embedded systems, robotics, and machine learning. I use GitHub to
document my learning, share engineering projects, and develop skills
through hands-on experimentation.

-   🎓 **Education:** B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering ---
    BRAC University
-   🔬 **Academic focus:** Electronics, VLSI, semiconductor devices, and
    circuit design
-   🤖 **Exploring:** Robotics, embedded systems, and machine learning
-   🌱 **Approach:** Build, test, document, and keep learning
-   🤝 **Interested in:** Engineering internships, research
    opportunities, and technical collaboration
