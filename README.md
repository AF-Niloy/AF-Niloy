## About

I’m **Abrar Faiyaz Niloy**, an Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at **BRAC University**, majoring in **Electronics** with a minor in **Power**.

- 🤝 **Looking for:** Engineering internships, research opportunities, and technical collaboration
